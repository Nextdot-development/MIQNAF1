Dual Ribosomal Binding

Mechanism of Action

Nafithromycin anchors at two ribosomal sites at once — so neither erm(B) methylation nor mef(A) efflux can switch it off.

I
Domain II Binding

Defeats erm(B) methylase resistance

The pyridyl-thiadiazole arm contacts the L22 protein at domain II of 23S rRNA — a site unaffected by A2058 methylation. Active even when domain V is blocked.5

II
Domain V Binding

Defeats mef(A) efflux resistance

Classical desosamine binding at domain V is retained. The dual-anchor affinity stays below the efflux pump’s clearance threshold, keeping activity against mef(A) isolates.3

III
vs. Classical Macrolides

Neither gene overcomes both sites

Azithromycin binds domain V only — one methylation or efflux event ends all activity. MIQNAF’s two contacts are additive: block one, the other holds.3,5
