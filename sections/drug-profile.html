Drug Profile

Indication and Uses

Dosage form and strength

MIQNAF® (nafithromycin) tablet is a white, oval shaped, biconvex, film-coated, immediate release 400 mg tablet for oral use. The tablet is debossed with ‘W755’ on one side and plain on other side.

Therapeutic indication

MIQNAF® is a macrolide class of antibacterial drug indicated for the treatment of adults (≥ 18 years of age) with community-acquired bacterial pneumonia (CABP).

Nafithromycin has been shown to be active against most strains of the following microorganisms, both in vitro and in clinical infections:

Aerobic typical Gram-positive bacteria

Streptococcus pneumoniae (including macrolide-resistant, penicillin-resistant, clindamycin-resistant and multi-drug resistant isolates [MDRSP]), Streptococcus anginosus, Streptococcus mitis/oralis, Staphylococcus aureus.

Aerobic typical Gram-negative fastidious bacteria

Haemophilus influenzae and Haemophilus parainfluenzae (both including macrolide-resistant, penicillin-resistant, clindamycin-resistant, trimethoprim/sulfamethoxazole-resistant and moxifloxacin-resistant isolates), Haemophilus parahaemolyticus, Moraxella catarrhalis.

Atypical bacteria

Mycoplasma pneumoniae, Chlamydophila pneumoniae, Legionella pneumophila.

Usage

To reduce the development of drug-resistant bacteria and maintain the effectiveness of MIQNAF® and other antibacterial drugs, MIQNAF® should be used only to treat infections that are proven or strongly suspected to be caused by susceptible bacteria. When culture and susceptibility information are available, they should be used to choose or alter antibacterial therapy. In the absence of such data, local epidemiology and susceptibility patterns may guide the empiric selection of therapy.

Posology

For the treatment of adults (≥ 18 years of age) with CABP, the recommended dosage regimen of MIQNAF® tablets is as follows:

  • Administer 800 mg (2 tablets of 400 mg each) orally, once-daily at every 24 hour interval for 3 days. The tablets should preferably be taken after a meal.

Method of administration. Each 800 mg dose of MIQNAF® consists of two 400 mg tablets. Two tablets of MIQNAF® should be taken orally, once every 24 hours, preferably after a meal. Swallow the MIQNAF® tablets sequentially, with enough water. Do not crush or chew the tablets.

To be sold against the prescription of a medical specialist only.
