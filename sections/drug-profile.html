Drug Profile

Indication and Uses

From the prescribing information for MIQNAF® (nafithromycin tablets).

Dosage form and strength

MIQNAF® (nafithromycin) tablet is a white, oval shaped, biconvex, film-coated, immediate release 400 mg tablet for oral use. The tablet is debossed with ‘W755’ on one side and plain on the other side.

Therapeutic indication

MIQNAF® is a macrolide class of antibacterial drug indicated for the treatment of adults (≥ 18 years of age) with community-acquired bacterial pneumonia (CABP).

Organisms

Nafithromycin has been shown to be active against most strains of the following microorganisms, both in vitro and in clinical infections.

Aerobic typical Gram-positive

  • Streptococcus pneumoniaeincluding macrolide-resistant, penicillin-resistant, clindamycin-resistant and multi-drug resistant isolates (MDRSP)
  • Streptococcus anginosus
  • Streptococcus mitis/oralis
  • Staphylococcus aureus

Aerobic typical Gram-negative fastidious

  • Haemophilus influenzaeincluding macrolide-, penicillin-, clindamycin-, trimethoprim/sulfamethoxazole- and moxifloxacin-resistant isolates
  • Haemophilus parainfluenzae
  • Haemophilus parahaemolyticus
  • Moraxella catarrhalis

Atypical

  • Mycoplasma pneumoniae
  • Chlamydophila pneumoniae
  • Legionella pneumophila

Usage

To reduce the development of drug-resistant bacteria and maintain the effectiveness of MIQNAF® and other antibacterial drugs, MIQNAF® should be used only to treat infections that are proven or strongly suspected to be caused by susceptible bacteria. When culture and susceptibility information are available, they should be used to choose or alter antibacterial therapy. In the absence of such data, local epidemiology and susceptibility patterns may guide the empiric selection of therapy.

Posology

For the treatment of adults (≥ 18 years of age) with CABP, the recommended dosage regimen of MIQNAF® tablets is as follows:

  • Administer 800 mg (2 tablets of 400 mg each) orally, once daily at every 24 hour interval for 3 days. The tablets should preferably be taken after a meal.

Method of administration. Each 800 mg dose consists of two 400 mg tablets, taken orally once every 24 hours, preferably after a meal. Swallow the tablets sequentially, with enough water. Do not crush or chew the tablets.
