Elderly

Elderly patients are at higher risk of rapid disease progression and complications. Miqnaf supports early clinical improvement and timely recovery.3

Comorbid

CABP can exacerbate respiratory (asthma/COPD) and cardio metabolic comorbidities, leading to hospitalization. Miqnaf provides comprehensive coverage, early clinical response and reassured safety1,3

Hospitalized

In moderate to severe hospitalized CABP, early clinical stabilization, shorter hospital stay, atypical coverage and safety remain key treatment goals. Miqnaf provides potent activity against atypical pathogens, enabling early clinical improvement with better safety over traditional macrolides.1,3
